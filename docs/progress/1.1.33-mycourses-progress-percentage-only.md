The course card's progress row rendered two competing metrics side by side —
`7%` and `5 / 67`. Requested: keep the percentage, drop the fraction.

`mc-card__progress-count` span from the progress head, plus its now-orphaned
SCSS block. No references to `progress-count` remain in `src/`.

feed the "قيد التعلم" / "مكتملة" filter counts at the top of the page. They are
simply no longer rendered on the card.
