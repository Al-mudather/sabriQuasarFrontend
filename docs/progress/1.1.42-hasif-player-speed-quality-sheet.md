# 1.1.42 — Al-Hasif player: Speed and Quality buttons, bottom sheet, double-tap seek

- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Status:** 🟡 partial — on `master` (not pushed). Driven for real in headless Chrome with phone and desktop profiles against a local 3-rendition HLS stream. Not tried on a physical phone, on iOS Safari, or against a production (AES-128) stream.
- **Commit:** `bb4b060`

## Problem

On phones the control bar folded mute, speed and quality into one "⋮" button that
opened a single long menu. Students did not find speed or quality there, and the
menu was taller than the screen.

## What changed

| Area | Change | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow bar (≤ 680px wide) | Row 2: play, −10, +10, mute (own button), fullscreen. Row 3: two equal buttons, **السرعة** and **الجودة**, each with icon, label and current value («عادي», «1.5×», «تلقائي · 720p», «720p»). No "⋮". Quality is hidden with a single rendition and Speed takes the row. | `ClassroomVideoControls.vue` |
| Wide bar | Same two buttons inline as chips (icon + label + value), volume slider kept. | same |
| Options list | One component, two looks: bottom sheet (touch, bar ≤ 520px, viewport ≤ 500px tall) or anchored popover (mouse). Title, rows ≥ 48px (sheet) with a check on the active one, max 60vh and scroll, safe-area padding, × button. `role=dialog`, radio rows, focus trap, focus returns to the button. | `PlayerOptionsPanel.vue` |
| Fullscreen | The app has no Quasar AppFullscreen plugin, so body-teleported menus are not painted in fullscreen. The sheet renders in `<body>` normally and inside the player shell while fullscreen. The auto-hiding bar stays while a list is open. | `ClassroomVideoControls.vue`, `TypeHasifHlsPlayer.vue` |
| Closing | Select, backdrop, Esc, ×, and the phone's Back (one history entry, swallowed before vue-router sees it). | `ClassroomVideoControls.vue` |
| Quality switch speed | A manual pick now ends with a disabled → enabled toggle of the chosen rendition, which is the only thing that makes VHS drop its forward buffer. Before, the picture changed when the old buffer ran out (25 s measured). | `useVideoJsPlayer.ts` |
| Remembered choices | Speed and the quality lock (by height) are kept in memory for the visit; the classroom mounts a new player per Hasif lesson, so they used to reset on every lesson. A reload starts from defaults. | same |
| Double-tap seek | Touch only: two taps on the left / right half seek −10 / +10 s (more taps add 10 s each) with an indicator. Left is "back" in Arabic too. A lone tap acts after 250 ms. Mouse click and keyboard shortcuts are unchanged. In fullscreen a tap on hidden controls only reveals them (it used to also toggle play on touch). | `TypeHasifHlsPlayer.vue`, `src/utils/playerControls.ts` |
| Bidi | `<body>` is LTR in this app (layouts carry `dir`), so the sheet sets its own `dir`; values are isolated with `dir="auto"`. | both components |
| Lesson-list button | On phones the Hasif player gets bottom room so the new row is never under the floating button. | `ClassroomContentView.vue` (CSS only) |

Deviation from the brief: the stacked layout applies up to 680px, not 520px. A
single row with both labelled buttons and the volume slider needs about 655px.
In the stacked layout the value sits under the label, not beside it:
«تلقائي · 1080p» does not fit beside «الجودة» in a 141px button.

## Verified (headless Chrome 141 UA, Playwright, real taps)

Profiles: 320×568, 360×640, 375×812 portrait; 812×375 landscape, windowed and
fullscreen; 1280×800 and 1680×950 desktop; English at 360×640 and 1280×800;
single-rendition stream at 360×640. All checks pass in every profile.

- Speed: picking 1.5× sets `video.playbackRate` to 1.5; it stays 1.5 through quality switches; 2× picked in fullscreen gives 2.
- Quality: 1080p → `videoHeight` 360 → 1080 in about 1.05 s, only that level enabled; 720p in about 0.63 s; auto → all three enabled and the label reads «تلقائي · 720p».
- Next Hasif lesson: rate 2, 360p lock re-applied, labels «2×» and «360p».
- Sheet at 360×640: panel 256–640px, rows 52px; in fullscreen its parent is the shell; controls still visible after 4 s idle with the sheet open, hidden again after closing.
- Back with the sheet open: sheet closes, same URL, still playing, no GraphQL request.
- Double-tap left: 41.0 s → 32.4 s while playing at 2×; three taps right: 33.9 s → 55.3 s; a lone tap pauses after the window (playing at 120 ms, paused at 620 ms).
- Tap targets: smallest control 44×44px. No horizontal scroll and no clipped label at 320px.
- Gates: `npx tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json`, jest (13 suites, 194 tests; `App.spec.js` and `login.spec.js` fail on `master` too), `npm run build`. The changed files also pass `vue-tsc` with an ad-hoc config.

## Not verified

- **Production streams.** They are AES-128 HLS (not ClearKey / EME); the test stream is unencrypted. The quality mechanism is the same VHS path.
- **iOS Safari.** It plays HLS natively: no quality levels are exposed, so the Quality button is hidden and Speed takes the row. iPhone has no element fullscreen, so the fullscreen button opens the system player and our controls (and sheets) are not shown there. Neither was run on a device.
- **A physical Android phone**, including the real Back gesture and safe-area insets.
